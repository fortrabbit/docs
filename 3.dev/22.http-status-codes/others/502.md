## Where a 502 comes from

| Seen | Likely source |
| -- | -- |
| A Cloudflare or CDN error page | The CDN could not get a valid answer from fortrabbit. Check the domain's DNS and TLS settings in [Cloudflare](/4.integrations/02.dns/05.cloudflare.md) |
| The fortrabbit 502 page | A part of the platform got no valid answer from the environment |
| The app's own error page | The app calls another service that failed, and passes the 502 on |

A PHP process that crashes or runs too long answers with a [503](/3.dev/22.http-status-codes/503.md) or a [504](/3.dev/22.http-status-codes/504.md). Check those pages first when the app itself is the suspect.

## What to check

- The [logs](/1.platform/07.settings/25.logs.md) of the environment at the time of the error.
- The [metrics](/1.platform/07.settings/30.metrics.md): a 502 together with OOM kills or a jump in PHP response time points to the app.

When the 502 stays and the logs show nothing, check the [fortrabbit status page](https://fortrabbit.betteruptime.com/) for incidents.
