## About external domains

fortrabbit does not register domains. Register the domain with a [domain provider](/4.integrations/02.dns/01.intro.md), add it to an environment in the dashboard, then point its DNS records to fortrabbit. The [external domain setup](/1.platform/13.domains/04.external-domain-setup.md) has the steps. Each correctly routed domain gets a free [TLS certificate](/1.platform/13.domains/06.https.md).

An external domain can move to another environment of the same app without DNS changes.

## Wildcard domains

Wildcard domains are not supported. Contact support with the use case.

## E-mail

E-mail for the domain runs through its MX records, set with the domain provider. fortrabbit does not touch them. See [e-mail hosting](/4.integrations/05.email/01.intro.md).
