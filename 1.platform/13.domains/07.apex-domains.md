## Apex routing at fortrabbit

Apex domains can not be routed to environments directly. Use a www domain: the [www forwarding service](/1.platform/13.domains/05.domain-forwarding-service.md) will redirect all requests.

## No CNAME for apex domains

The [DNS standard](https://www.ietf.org/rfc/rfc1035.txt) allows no other records next to a CNAME, so a CNAME on the apex would break its MX records and with them e-mail like `info@example.com`. Some DNS providers offer ANAME or ALIAS records to work around this.

## No A record routing to environments

A host name can point somewhere else at any time, an IP address cannot. fortrabbit routes domains by host name, so environments can move during scaling or an incident, such as a DDoS attack, without DNS changes on the customer side.

## Arguments

An apex domain looks cleaner, but it does not fit routing by host name. Large sites such as Google serve from `www.`, and Safari and Chrome hide the prefix in the address bar. Moving from the apex to `www.` does not hurt search rankings when the apex forwards every request, deep links included, with a 301. People can keep using the apex domain on business cards and in posts.
