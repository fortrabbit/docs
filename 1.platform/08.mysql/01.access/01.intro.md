## Get ready

The environment needs the [MySQL component](/1.platform/09.components/02.mysql.md). It is optional, and the presets for database software book it.

From a local machine, connect with `mysql` and `mysqldump` from a [local development environment](/4.integrations/01.local-development/01.intro.md), or with a [database GUI](/4.integrations/06.database-clients/01.intro.md) such as Sequel Ace or MySQL Workbench. In a containerized setup, the tools may run inside the container.
