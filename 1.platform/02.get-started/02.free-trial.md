## Starting a new trial

Create a new app in the dashboard. Each new app starts as a new trial.

## Use cases

The flexible free trial allows a variety of use cases and workflows. Fair usage of provided resources is expected.

- Exploring the platform
- Feature development
- Handover work to a client
- Weekend experiments

## Trial time limit

The trial lasts seven days. A countdown in the dashboard shows the time left.

## Trial threshold

An app on [larger plans](/1.platform/02.get-started/03.choosing-a-hosting-plan.md) can have a shorter trial. The largest plans need a payment method on the app, also during the trial.

## Extend trial time

:ContactUs{text="Ask support for more trial time"} before the trial ends, with a few words on the project and why it needs longer. Most requests are granted.

## Trial end

What happens at the end of the trial depends on the payment method:

### Without a payment method

- The default, no credit card required.
- The app is **deleted** when the trial ends.
- Add a payment method before the end to keep it.

### With a payment method

- A [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md) on the app, with valid payment details. Nothing is charged during the trial.
- Billing starts when the trial ends.
- Delete the app before the end to pay nothing.

## DELETE on trial end

Without a payment method, the app, all its environments and their data are deleted when the trial ends. An e-mail announces the end ahead of time.

Keep the code in Git and a [local development environment](/4.integrations/01.local-development/01.intro.md) running. A new trial can start right away, and the code deploys again.

## Free trial limits

All [components](/1.platform/09.components/index.md) can be booked during the trial, in all sizes. The largest plans need a payment method, see the [trial threshold](#trial-threshold).
