## About jobs memory

Every five minutes the highest memory use of all [workers](/1.platform/30.jobs/03.workers.md) and [crons](/1.platform/30.jobs/02.crons.md) is recorded. Each bar shows the lowest value, the mean measure, and the highest value of its hour or day. The mean measure is the 99th percentile by default; switch it to the median or the average in the options. Horizontal lines mark the jobs plan sizes.

:BlockLink{title="See jobs memory for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/jobs-memory"}

## What to look for

Workers are long-running PHP processes. Memory that climbs steadily and only drops on a restart points to a leak: each processed job leaves something behind. Short peaks line up with cron schedules or large jobs. At the plan limit, a process is killed and counted under [jobs OOM kills](/1.platform/31.metrics/14.jobs-oom-kills.md).

## What to do

Restart workers after a number of jobs or at a memory limit. Most queue libraries have an option for it. Split large jobs into batches. [Tune jobs](/1.platform/30.jobs/05.tuning.md) covers both. Scale the [Jobs component](/1.platform/09.components/07.jobs.md#scaling) when the usage is legitimate.
