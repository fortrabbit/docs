## About 503 errors

The load balancer counts every response with status 503, for PHP and static files alike. Each bar counts its hour or day. The summary shows the total of the time frame and the highest bar. The table lists the URLs by count.

:BlockLink{title="See 503 errors for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/error-503"}

## What to look for

A 503 means a request found no free PHP process, or PHP was down. Spikes together with high [PHP requests](/1.platform/31.metrics/09.php-requests.md) or [PHP response time](/1.platform/31.metrics/08.php-response-time.md) mean all processes were busy. Spikes together with [OOM kills](/1.platform/31.metrics/12.php-oom-kills.md) mean PHP ran out of memory.

## What to do

Speed up slow requests first: a process that answers faster serves more requests. Cache pages where possible and block unwanted bots. When the load is real, scale the [PHP component](/1.platform/09.components/01.php.md#scaling) for more processes. See [troubleshoot 503 errors](/3.dev/22.http-status-codes/503.md).
