## About jobs OOM kills

When workers and crons together need more memory than the jobs plan provides, the system kills a process to free memory. Each bar counts the kills of its hour or day. The summary shows the total of the time frame.

:BlockLink{title="See jobs OOM kills for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/jobs-oom"}

## What to look for

The healthy value is zero. A killed process stops mid-job. Depending on the queue, the job is retried, lost, or left half done. Kills at fixed times point to a cron, kills spread over the day to a worker that grows over time.

## What to do

Open [jobs memory](/1.platform/31.metrics/13.jobs-memory.md) for the same time frame and check the [jobs logs](/1.platform/07.settings/25.logs.md#jobs-logs) for the job that ran last. Process large data sets in batches and restart workers regularly, see [tune jobs](/1.platform/30.jobs/05.tuning.md). Scale the [Jobs component](/1.platform/09.components/07.jobs.md#scaling) when the job needs the memory.
