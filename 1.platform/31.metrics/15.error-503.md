## About 503 errors

The load balancer counts every response with status 503, for PHP and static files alike. The summary shows the total of the time frame.

:BlockLink{title="See 503 errors for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/error-503"}

## What to look for

A 503 means a request found no PHP process to handle it, or PHP was down. Spikes together with high [PHP requests](/1.platform/31.metrics/07.php-requests.md) or [PHP response time](/1.platform/31.metrics/06.php-response-time.md) mean all processes were busy. Spikes together with [OOM kills](/1.platform/31.metrics/10.php-oom-kills.md) mean PHP ran out of memory.

## What to do

Speed up slow requests first: a process that answers faster serves more requests. Cache pages where possible and block unwanted bots. When the load is real, scale the [PHP component](/1.platform/09.components/01.php.md#scaling) to get more processes. See [troubleshoot 503 errors](/3.dev/22.http-status-codes/503.md).
