## About metrics

Metrics are measured on the platform, outside the app. No code, agent or extension is needed. Values are sampled every 5 minutes and grouped into bars.

:BlockLink{title="See metrics for {{app-name}} / {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics"}

## Time frame and resolution

The time frame selector sets the range and what a bar stands for:

| Time frame                        | One bar is |
| --------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Last 2 days                       | One hour   |
| Last 30, 60 or 90 days            | One day    |
| Previous two calendar months      | One day    |
| Custom range                      | One day    |

All times are UTC.

## Reading the bars

Metrics come in two chart types:

- **Stacked**: counts and sizes, such as requests, traffic or storage. A bar shows the total for its hour or day, split into segments where the metric has more than one source.
- **Layered**: measurements spread over many requests, such as PHP response time and memory. A bar shows the lowest value, the 99th percentile and the highest value for its hour or day. The 99th percentile is the value 99% of all samples stay below. It ignores rare outliers and still catches the slow tail most visitors feel.

Horizontal lines mark plan sizes or thresholds. The figures next to the chart summarize the whole time frame.

## Metrics, logs and APM

Metrics show that something changed and when. [Logs](/1.platform/07.settings/25.logs.md) show the single request behind it. An [APM tool](/4.integrations/04.apm/01.intro.md) traces a request through the code. Start with the metric, narrow the time frame, then read the logs for that window.
