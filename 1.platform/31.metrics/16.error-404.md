## About 404 errors

The load balancer counts every response with status 404, for PHP and static files alike. Each bar counts its hour or day. The summary shows the total of the time frame and the highest bar. The table lists the URLs by count.

:BlockLink{title="See 404 errors for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/error-404"}

## What to look for

Every site gets 404s: old links, typos, and bots probing for `wp-login.php` or `.env`. A spike after a deployment or relaunch points to moved pages without redirects, or broken asset paths. A spike without a deployment usually is a bot scanning the site.

## What to do

The table names the missing URLs. Add redirects for moved pages and fix broken asset links. Probing bots need no fix, but each of their requests that reaches PHP still takes a process. See [troubleshoot 404 errors](/3.dev/22.http-status-codes/404.md).
