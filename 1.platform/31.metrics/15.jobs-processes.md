## About jobs processes

Each bar is the highest number of job processes running at once in its hour or day. The summary shows the highest and lowest of the time frame. Horizontal lines mark process counts.

:BlockLink{title="See jobs processes for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/jobs-processes"}

## What to look for

Workers run all the time, so their count forms a flat base. Crons add short peaks on their schedule. When the count sits at the plan limit for long, new jobs wait for a free process and the queue grows. Peaks that overlap often mean several crons start at the same minute.

## What to do

Spread cron schedules so they do not start together. Check [jobs memory](/1.platform/31.metrics/13.jobs-memory.md) before adding workers, every process needs its own memory. Scale the [Jobs component](/1.platform/09.components/07.jobs.md#scaling) when the queue keeps growing.
