## About 500 errors

The load balancer counts every response with status 500, for PHP and static files alike. The summary shows the total of the time frame.

:BlockLink{title="See 500 errors for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/error-500"}

## What to look for

Any 500 is a bug or a misconfiguration: an uncaught exception, a missing environment variable, a database that cannot be reached. A spike right after a deployment points to the new code.

## What to do

Filter the [PHP logs](/1.platform/07.settings/25.logs.md#php-logs) by level `ERROR` for the time of the spike. The fatal error and its stack trace name the file and line. Scaling does not help here. See [troubleshoot 500 errors](/3.dev/22.http-status-codes/500.md).
