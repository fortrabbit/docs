## About OPcache

The chart shows the fill level of the OPcache memory in percent. Each bar is the highest value sampled in its hour or day. The summary shows the current value and the highest of the time frame. A line marks 100%.

:BlockLink{title="See PHP OPcache for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/php-opcache"}

## What to look for

The fill level rises after a deployment as files get compiled, then stays flat. It depends on the amount of PHP code, including everything in `vendor/`, not on the number of visitors. A fill level at 100% means the cache is full. PHP then compiles the files that do not fit on every request, which costs response time.

## What to do

Remove unused Composer packages and CMS plugins, then deploy again. Run `composer install --no-dev` in [build commands](/1.platform/06.deployment/03.build-commands.md) so development dependencies stay out of production. :ContactUs{text="Contact us"} when the OPcache stays full after that.
