## About 404 errors

The load balancer counts every response with status 404, for PHP and static files alike. The summary shows the total of the time frame.

:BlockLink{title="See 404 errors for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/error-404"}

## What to look for

Every site gets 404s: old links, typos, bots probing for `wp-login.php` or `.env`. A spike after a deployment or relaunch points to moved pages without redirects or broken asset paths. A spike without a deployment usually is a bot scanning the site.

## What to do

Filter the [access logs](/1.platform/07.settings/25.logs.md#access-logs) by status `404` to see which URLs fail. Add redirects for moved pages and fix broken asset links. Probing bots need no fix, but each request that reaches PHP still costs a process. See [troubleshoot 404 errors](/3.dev/22.http-status-codes/404.md).
