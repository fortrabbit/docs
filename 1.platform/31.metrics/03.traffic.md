## About traffic

The chart adds up incoming and outgoing bytes of all HTTP requests to the environment, PHP and static files alike. The summary shows the total of the time frame and the highest single bar.

:BlockLink{title="See Traffic for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/traffic"}

## What to look for

Traffic follows visitor numbers, but page weight drives it more. A site with large images or uncompressed assets transfers more per visit. A jump without more visitors points to a new heavy asset, a hotlinked file or a crawler downloading everything.

## What to do

Compare traffic with [PHP requests](/1.platform/31.metrics/07.php-requests.md) and [static requests](/1.platform/31.metrics/04.static-requests.md) for the same time frame. When requests stay flat while traffic rises, the responses got heavier: see [reduce page weight](/3.dev/02.performance/09.page-weight.md). Traffic is billed per month, see the [traffic component](/1.platform/09.components/04.traffic.md).
