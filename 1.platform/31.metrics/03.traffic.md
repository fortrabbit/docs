## About traffic

Each bar adds up the incoming and outgoing bytes of all HTTP requests in its hour or day, PHP and static files alike. The summary shows the total of the time frame and the highest bar. The table lists the URLs by traffic, with their request count.

:BlockLink{title="See traffic for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/traffic"}

## What to look for

Traffic follows visitor numbers, but page weight drives it more. A site with large images or uncompressed assets transfers more per visit. A jump without more visitors points to a new heavy file, a hotlinked image, or a crawler downloading everything. The top rows of the table name the cause.

## What to do

Shrink or compress the files at the top of the table, see [reduce page weight](/3.dev/02.performance/09.page-weight.md). Compare with [PHP requests](/1.platform/31.metrics/09.php-requests.md) and [static requests](/1.platform/31.metrics/04.static-requests.md): flat requests with rising traffic mean heavier responses. Traffic is billed per month, see the [traffic component](/1.platform/09.components/04.traffic.md).
