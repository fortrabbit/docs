## About OOM kills

When the PHP processes together need more memory than the PHP plan provides, the system kills a process to free memory. Each bar counts the kills of its hour or day. The summary shows the total of the time frame.

:BlockLink{title="See OOM kills for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/frontend-oom"}

## What to look for

The healthy value is zero. A kill ends the request the process was handling, and the visitor gets an error page. Single kills often come from one heavy request, such as an export or an image upload. Repeated kills at busy times mean the plan is too small for the load.

## What to do

Open [PHP memory](/1.platform/31.metrics/10.php-memory.md) for the same time frame and find the peak. Check the [logs](/1.platform/07.settings/25.logs.md) for the requests at that time. Fix the request that loads too much, or scale the [PHP component](/1.platform/09.components/01.php.md#scaling) to a plan with more memory.
