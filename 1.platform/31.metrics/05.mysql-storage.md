## About MySQL storage

Each bar is the highest used storage of the database in its hour or day. The summary shows the current value and the highest and lowest of the time frame. Horizontal lines mark the MySQL plan sizes.

:BlockLink{title="See MySQL storage for {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics/mysql-storage"}

## What to look for

A database grows with content. Fast growth usually comes from tables nobody reads: sessions, cache entries, search indexes, revisions, or plugin logs. A crawler that creates entries, for example through a search form, can fill a table within days.

## What to do

List the largest tables in a [MySQL client](/1.platform/08.mysql/01.access/01.intro.md), then clean up or limit what fills them. The [MySQL component](/1.platform/09.components/02.mysql.md#scaling) covers common causes. When the growth is real, scale the MySQL plan or turn on [autoscaling](/1.platform/12.billing/11.autoscaling.md).
