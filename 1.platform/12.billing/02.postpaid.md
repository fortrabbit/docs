## How prepaid works

Most hosting providers bill in advance for the coming period, often with yearly contracts paid monthly. That locks customers in and secures the revenue of the provider.

## How postpaid works

Postpaid, also called billing in arrears, charges after use, for each [monthly service period](/1.platform/12.billing/05.schedule.md):

- Nothing is charged on booking, the first invoice follows the first month.
- After a deletion, the last days appear on the next invoice.

## Postpaid benefits

Postpaid makes [billing by the day](/1.platform/12.billing/03.daily-aggregation.md) possible: an environment for a few days of testing costs a few days.

## No yearly payments

Postpaid does not fit yearly payments, which would need to know the usage of the coming year in advance.
