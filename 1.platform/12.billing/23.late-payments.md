## Why payments bounce

Common reasons:

- Not enough funds: the retry a few days later may work.
- An expired card: [update the payment details](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md#changing-payment-credentials).
- The bank declines: update the payment details or ask the bank.

An e-mail announces every failed payment. :ContactUs{text="Contact us"} with questions about an invoice.

## Automatic retries

Failed payments are retried during the month. About half of them go through on a retry.

## Service cancellation

Invoices that stay unpaid lead to the deletion of the apps. A person reviews each case before. **Deleted apps cannot be recovered.** The [blog post on bounced payments](https://blog.fortrabbit.com/bounced-payment) explains why.

## App deletion policy

The rule depends on the payment history:

- **New clients**: when the first or second invoice bounces and the e-mails get no answer, the apps are deleted.
- **Fewer than 6 paid invoices**: the apps may be deleted after a bounce, also when the payment provider flags possible fraud.
- **More than 6 paid invoices**: after 2 bounced invoices in a row, or 4 unpaid in total, fortrabbit also tries to reach someone in person. Without an answer, the apps may be deleted.
- **Invoices above €1,000 a month**: handled individually.

## Paying open invoices

The dashboard shows a warning with the open invoices and how to pay them. Card payments go through at once, SEPA direct debits take a few days.

## Refund policy

**fortrabbit does not refund invoices.** Booked components use resources whether or not the website gets visitors. For an error on an invoice, :ContactUs{text="contact support"}, which can credit an amount to future invoices.

## Additional fees for bounced payments

The bank charges fortrabbit a fee for each failed payment. fortrabbit does not pass it on.
