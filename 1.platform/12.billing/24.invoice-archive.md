## Download from the dashboard

Open the payment method in the dashboard to see its invoices. It needs access to the payment method, and the payment method must still exist: deleting it deletes the archive. Each invoice opens in the browser and downloads as PDF.

## Find invoices in your email

Each invoice e-mail contains a private link to the invoice, to print or download it. Search the inbox for "fortrabbit invoice".
