Stripe, the payment provider, sets the minimum: around 50 cents, depending on currency and [payment type](/1.platform/12.billing/10.payment-types.md). An [invoice](/1.platform/10.objects/07.invoice.md) below it is set to zero, so nothing is due that month. The amount moves to the balance of the payment method and is added to the next invoice.

## Example

```raw
€0.30  Invoice March  -> below the minimum, moved to the balance
€4.50  Invoice April  + €0.30 from the balance
€4.80  Due in April
```
