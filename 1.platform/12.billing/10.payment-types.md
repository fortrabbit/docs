Set the payment type in the [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md). Credit cards work everywhere, SEPA direct debit with a bank account in the EU. Which types show up depends on the country.

:PaymentProvidersFeatured{size="md"}

## Data sharing

Payment details go directly to Stripe, the payment provider, and never pass through fortrabbit.
