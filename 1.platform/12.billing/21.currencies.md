## Ruleset

The country of the [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md) sets the currency:

| Country | Currency |
| -- | -- |
| EU countries, United Kingdom | EUR |
| Switzerland, Norway | EUR or USD |
| All other countries | USD |

## Changing the currency

The currency of a payment method cannot be changed later. Create a new payment method with the other currency and move the apps to it.
