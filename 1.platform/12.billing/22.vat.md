## Fiscal implications

Prices are net, without VAT. fortrabbit is based in Germany, which sets the rules:

| Country of the payment method | With a valid VAT ID | Without |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Germany | German VAT | German VAT |
| Other EU member states | No VAT, reverse charge | VAT of that country |
| United Kingdom | UK VAT | UK VAT |
| Non-EU countries | No VAT | No VAT |

## Countries

### EU member states

Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden.

### United Kingdom

The UK is not part of the EU. fortrabbit is registered for UK VAT and adds it to every invoice. UK businesses can't add a VAT ID yet.

### Non-EU countries

All other countries, including the European countries outside the EU, such as Switzerland, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein. Invoices show no VAT and no VAT ID is needed. Any tax due in the customer's country, such as a reverse charge for businesses, is handled there.

## VATIN for EU clients

Businesses in EU member states other than Germany add their VAT identification number (VAT ID) to the payment method, see [adding a VAT IN](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md#adding-a-vat-in). It applies from the next invoice.

## EU VAT exemption

International bodies, embassies and other organizations with a VAT exemption certificate under article 151(1)(a) of the VAT Directive can be exempt. :ContactUs{text="Contact us"} with the certificate.
