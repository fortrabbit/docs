## Plan resources

Each plan sets the resources of a [component](/1.platform/03.concepts/03.components.md): memory and PHP processes for PHP, storage for MySQL and web storage, monthly volume for traffic. The component pages and the [pricing page](https://www.fortrabbit.com/pricing) list them.

## Tiers

Plans grow in steps wide enough that most websites stay on one plan from month to month, see [tiers](/1.platform/12.billing/30.tiers.md).

## Changing plans

Scale a plan up or down at any time, with immediate effect and [billed by the day](/1.platform/12.billing/03.daily-aggregation.md), for example before a campaign or after a busy season.
