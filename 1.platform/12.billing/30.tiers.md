The plans of the components that [autoscale](/1.platform/12.billing/11.autoscaling.md) grow in wide steps. The smallest [traffic](/1.platform/09.components/04.traffic.md) plan includes 32 GiB a month, enough for most websites. With autoscaling on, most environments stay on the same plan month after month.
