## About metrics

Metrics are measured on the platform, outside the app. No code, agent, or extension is needed. Values are sampled every five minutes and grouped into bars.

:BlockLink{title="See metrics for {{app-name}} / {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/metrics"}

## Time frame and resolution

The time frame sets the range and what one bar stands for. Times are UTC.

| Time frame           | One bar |
| -------------------- | ------- |
| Last 2 days          | Hour    |
| Last 30, 60, 90 days | Day     |
| Previous two months  | Day     |
| Custom range         | Day     |

## Reading the charts

Counts and sizes, such as requests, traffic, or storage, show as stacked bars. A bar holds the total of its hour or day.

Measurements spread over many samples, such as PHP response time and memory, show as layered bars: the lowest value, the mean measure, and the highest value. The mean measure is the 99th percentile by default, the value 99% of samples stay below. It ignores rare outliers and still catches the slow tail most visitors feel. Switch it to the median or the average in the options.

Horizontal lines mark plan sizes or thresholds. The figures next to the chart summarize the whole time frame. Request and error metrics add a table that names the URLs behind the numbers.

## Metrics, logs, and APM

Metrics show that something changed, and when. [Logs](/1.platform/07.settings/25.logs.md) show the single request behind it. An [APM tool](/4.integrations/04.apm/01.intro.md) traces a request through the code. Start with the metric, narrow the time frame, then read the logs for that window.
