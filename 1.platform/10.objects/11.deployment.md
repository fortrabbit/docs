A deployment shows its state, the environment, the commit with a link to GitHub, how it was triggered, and the [deploy log](/1.platform/06.deployment/17.logs.md), which is kept for 10 days. The dashboard lists the deployments of all apps, with filters.

A deployment starts with a push through the [fortrabbit GitHub App](/1.platform/06.deployment/02.github-app.md), the Deploy now button, or a [deploy hook](/1.platform/06.deployment/16.deploy-hook.md). Files copied by SSH, SFTP or rsync, for example from GitHub Actions, are no deployments and do not show up.

See the [deployment intro](/1.platform/06.deployment/01.intro.md) for the steps of a deployment.
