## Creating an app

The dashboard wizard sets up an app step by step: Git repository, software template, region, plans and payment method. The region cannot be changed later.

:BlockLink{title="Create an app" path="/new/app/"}

## Apps and environments

An app maps to a Git repository, and each of its [environments](/1.platform/10.objects/02.environment.md) to a branch, with its own data, settings and components. Every app has at least one environment.

## Collaboration

Access is granted per app, see [collaboration](/1.platform/20.collaboration/1.intro.md). Developers on an app manage its environments and settings, deploy, and access the code.

## Billing

A [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md) pays for the app. Developers manage it directly or through a [team](/1.platform/10.objects/04.team.md).

## Deleting an app

Delete an app in its settings in the dashboard. All environments go with it, including their files and databases. Deletion cannot be undone.

## Preview image and links

In some lists, apps can show a preview image and a link to be visited in the browser. The image and the link will use the [environment](/1.platform/10.objects/02.environment.md) that is most likely production and its [main domain](/1.platform/07.settings/05.routing.md#main-domain).

## App name

The app name suggested at creation is the name of the Git repository. It can be changed at any time and serves as a label in the dashboard. Domains, logins and IDs do not depend on it. An optional description adds detail.

## Changing the Git repo of an app

Change the repository in the app settings. Each environment then needs a branch of the new repository.

## Changing the payment method of an app

Move an app to another payment method the account has access to. The old payment method pays up to the day of the change, the new one from the next day, see [post-paid billing](/1.platform/12.billing/02.postpaid.md). People with access to the new payment method gain control of the app.

## Technical contacts

[Technical contacts](/1.platform/20.collaboration/4.technical-contact.md) receive the technical e-mails about the app, such as warnings before a limit is reached.
