## Invoice details

Each [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md) with apps gets one invoice per month, after the month ends. It lists every app, its environments and their components.

- **Invoice number**
- **Invoice date**: the day it was issued
- **Service period**: the month it covers
- **Invoice address and e-mail**: from the payment method
- **VAT**: see [VAT](/1.platform/12.billing/22.vat.md)

## States

An invoice passes these states:

### Draft

During the month, the draft is updated daily and with each booking, and shows the costs so far.

### Paid

After the month ends, the invoice is finalized, sent, and charged to the payment method.

### Bounced

When the charge fails, the invoice is bounced. Several retries follow, and an invoice that stays unpaid leads to cancellation, see [late payments](/1.platform/12.billing/23.late-payments.md).

## Invoice item rows

Rows are grouped by environment. Each row names a [component](/1.platform/03.concepts/03.components.md) plan and the days it was booked. An example with made-up prices, where PHP XS was scaled to PHP SM after ten days:

| Component plan | Days billed | Daily price | Amount |
| -------------- | ----------: | ----------: | -----: |
| PHP XS         |          10 |       $0.10 |  $1.00 |
| PHP SM         |          20 |       $0.20 |  $4.00 |
| Traffic XS     |          30 |       $0.03 |  $0.90 |

See [post-paid billing](/1.platform/12.billing/02.postpaid.md). The PDF invoice is laid out differently.
