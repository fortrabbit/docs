## Recap

A [person](/1.platform/10.objects/03.person.md) has personal access, a [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md) stands for the party that pays, and a team groups developers who work on the same apps. See the [collaboration intro](/1.platform/20.collaboration/1.intro.md).

## Use cases

- A whole organization, such as an agency or a startup
- A unit within a business
- Any other group of developers

## Creating a team

Teams are free, and an account can create any number of them.

:BlockLink{title="Create a team" path="/new/team"}

## Inviting team members

See [invite to a team](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/01.invite-to-team.md). Each member has a role, see [team roles](/1.platform/20.collaboration/8.team-roles.md).

## Payment method access

A [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md) can belong to a team, so its members manage billing together, and the team owns the apps that payment method pays for.

## Deleting a team

Deleting a team also deletes the payment methods and apps that belong to nothing else. The dashboard lists them before the deletion. Personal accounts stay.

## Leaving a team

A member who leaves loses access to the apps of the team, in the dashboard and by SSH and SFTP. The team stays.

- **Leave**: the Leave button on the team overview in the dashboard. The last full member cannot leave, as a team needs one.
- **Remove**: a team member with the right role removes another one from the member list of the team.

Closing an account leaves all its teams.

## Several teams

An account can belong to any number of teams, with a different role in each. To work with the same person in several teams, invite them to each.
