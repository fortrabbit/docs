## Overview

- A payment method has a payment type, an invoice address and VAT settings.
- Each [app](/1.platform/10.objects/01.app.md) belongs to a payment method, except [during the trial](/1.platform/02.get-started/02.free-trial.md).
- A payment method can pay for many apps.
- Payment methods are managed by people directly or in [teams](/1.platform/10.objects/04.team.md)
- Each team or person can have access on multiple payment methods
- Each payment method has its own [invoice](/1.platform/10.objects/07.invoice.md) archive
- Payment methods are free of charge, apps they own cost something
- People are connected to payment methods by a [billing connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/5.billing-connection.md)

## Creating a payment method

Adding a payment method to a trial app creates one. A payment method can also be created on its own, for later use.

:BlockLink{ title="Create a new payment method" path="/new/payment-method" }

## Multiple payment methods

A payment method is not part of the account, so one account can use several, for example one per client. Each app is paid by one of them.

## Shared payment methods

A payment method can be shared with a team or a client, see [invite to a payment method](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/11.invite-to-payment-method.md) and [invite to pay](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/12.invite-to-pay.md).

## Changing the invoice address

The invoice address of the payment method appears on its invoices. Changes apply to future invoices.

## Changing payment credentials

Replace the card or the bank account of a payment method in the dashboard, for example when a card expires. The new details pay future invoices and any open ones. It needs an account with access to the payment method.

## Adding a VAT IN

Businesses registered in the EU add their VAT identification number (VAT IN), which starts with the country code. It is not the local tax number. fortrabbit validates it, and it changes the VAT on future invoices, not past ones. See [VAT](/1.platform/12.billing/22.vat.md).

## Changing the payment method of an app

Two ways:

- Replace the card or bank account of the current payment method. All its apps then use the new details.
- [Move the app](/1.platform/10.objects/01.app.md#changing-the-payment-method-of-an-app) to another payment method. Only that app changes, and the people with access to the new payment method gain control of it.

## Adding a promo code

Enter a promo code in the settings of the payment method. It gives a discount on future invoices of that payment method. Codes are case-sensitive, and a payment method holds one promo code, which cannot be removed or replaced later.

## Deleting a payment method

Deleting a payment method also deletes all its apps and its invoice archive, like a cancellation of the service.

## Troubleshooting setup issues

Payment details go straight to Stripe, the payment provider. When a card is declined:

- Complete the confirmation of the bank, such as a redirect or a push message on the phone. EU cards usually need it.
- Ask the bank whether it blocks the payment, for example for online or foreign payments.
- Try another card, or SEPA direct debit with an EU bank account.
