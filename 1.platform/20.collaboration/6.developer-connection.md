# Developer connection
person → app

## What a developer connection allows

- Open the full app overview and settings
- Create, change and delete environments
- Deploy and access the code by SSH and SFTP
- Change Git settings, with a direct connection or as an unlimited team member

Deleting the app and inviting other developers to it need a direct connection, the unlimited role in one of the app's teams, or a [billing connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/5.billing-connection.md).

## Who can have a developer connection?

Developers only, see [account types](/1.platform/20.collaboration/7.account-types.md). Clients never have one.

## Developer connection types

A developer can have a direct developer connection or one through a [team](/1.platform/10.objects/04.team.md).

### Direct developer connection

Personal apps belong to their developer, who can share each one by [app invite](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/09.invite-to-app.md).

### Team developer connection

A new app is created in the personal context or in a team. Team members are not connected to every app of the team: they subscribe to the apps they work on. Unlimited members subscribe themselves, limited members request access, see [team roles](/1.platform/20.collaboration/8.team-roles.md).

Unlimited members also see the overview of team apps they are not subscribed to, without the technical parts.

## One developer connection per app

A developer has one developer connection per app. When the app is reachable through several teams, or directly, the developer picks one.

## Relation to technical contacts

Only some connected developers need to be [technical contacts](/1.platform/20.collaboration/4.technical-contact.md).

## Relation to billing connection

The [billing connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/5.billing-connection.md) is the billing counterpart. A developer can hold both on the same app.
