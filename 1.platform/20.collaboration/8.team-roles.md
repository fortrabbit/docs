## Unlimited

The standard role, for all members of a small team or the leads of a large one. Full access to the team, its apps, its members and its billing:

- open the overview of every app of the team
- subscribe to any app of the team for a [developer connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/6.developer-connection.md), and unsubscribe
- invite developers to the team's apps
- delete apps of the team, connect them to other teams, or disconnect them from this one
- [billing connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/5.billing-connection.md) through every payment method connected to the team
- create, change and delete payment methods connected to the team
- manage all other member roles
- [invite new members](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/01.invite-to-team.md) for any role to the team
- leave the team (if other members are present)
- delete the team

## Limited

For developers outside the core team, such as freelancers or juniors:

- request access to team apps
- work on the apps they are connected to: settings, environments, deployments and code access
- unsubscribe from team apps
- leave the team

Limited members see no billing of the team. Inviting developers to an app needs a direct developer connection on it. Deleting the app or disconnecting it from the team needs a direct developer connection or a billing connection.

Clients cannot join teams, see [account types](/1.platform/20.collaboration/7.account-types.md).
