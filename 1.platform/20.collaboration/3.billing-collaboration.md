Teams and people share access to a payment method, which pays for the apps.

## Non technical client access

Business owners are often not developers. A client account hides the technical parts of the dashboard, see [account types](/1.platform/20.collaboration/7.account-types.md).

## Direct billing access

Developers and clients connect to a payment method directly by [invite](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/11.invite-to-payment-method.md). They then control the payment method and its apps.

## Billing access with teams

A payment method can belong to a team. Members with the unlimited [role](/1.platform/20.collaboration/8.team-roles.md) then have full access to it.

## Separation of concerns

Some agencies and freelancers bill hosting to their clients as part of a maintenance fee. That works. A client who pays fortrabbit directly, through [invite to pay](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/12.invite-to-pay.md), closes a gap in responsibility: fortrabbit deals with the client on billing and service, and the developer can step away from an app without moving it.

fortrabbit offers no white-label hosting and no agency commissions.

Developer access works the same way, see [developer collaboration](/1.platform/20.collaboration/2.developer-collaboration.md).
