## Use cases

An app invite shares one app, without a team:

- A solo developer invites a colleague
- A loosely connected group of developers collaborating per project
- A team works together with another team and shares one app
- A team invites a freelancer to a specific app but not to the whole team
- A client invites a developer

## How it works

The inviter sends the invite, the invited developer accepts it.

### On the inviter side

1. Open the app invite in the dashboard.
2. Pick the [app](/1.platform/10.objects/01.app.md).
3. Enter the e-mail address and send.

:BlockLink{title="Invite to app" path="/invite/app"}

### On the invited side

1. An e-mail with an invite link arrives.
2. Log in to fortrabbit, or create an account.
3. Accept the invite.

### Consequences

- The invited developer gets a [developer connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/6.developer-connection.md), with the same access to the app as the inviter.
- They can change all settings, deploy, delete the app and invite others.
- Billing and the payment method of the app stay as they are.

## Alternative team invite

For regular work on several apps, a [team](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/01.invite-to-team.md) is easier.
