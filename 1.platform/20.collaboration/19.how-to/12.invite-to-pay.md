## Get ready

A [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md) pays for [apps](/1.platform/10.objects/01.app.md) and is controlled by the [people](/1.platform/10.objects/03.person.md) connected to it. See [billing collaboration](/1.platform/20.collaboration/3.billing-collaboration.md).

## Use cases

- A freelancer hands the apps to the client.
- A project changes owner.

The invited person is usually a business owner, who signs up as a client, see [account types](/1.platform/20.collaboration/7.account-types.md).

### Designed for freelancers and their clients

Invite to pay hands over ownership, and the inviter loses the [billing connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/5.billing-connection.md) to the apps. The client becomes responsible for the hosting, not the freelancer.

## How it works

The inviter sends the invite, the invited person accepts it.

### On the inviter side

1. Open invite to pay in the dashboard. Trial [apps](/1.platform/10.objects/01.app.md) work too.
2. Pick the apps to hand over.
3. Enter the e-mail address and send.

:BlockLink{title="Invite to pay" path="/invite/pay"}

### On the invited side

1. An e-mail with an invite link arrives.
2. Log in to fortrabbit, or create an account.
3. Accept the invite, and choose or create the payment method for the apps.

### Consequences

- The inviter loses the [billing connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/5.billing-connection.md) and keeps the [developer connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/6.developer-connection.md).
- The invited person can delete the apps and change their developers.

## Alternatives

To share an existing payment method instead, use [invite to a payment method](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/11.invite-to-payment-method.md).
