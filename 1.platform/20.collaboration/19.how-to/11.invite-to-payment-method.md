## Get ready

A [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md) pays for [apps](/1.platform/10.objects/01.app.md) and is controlled by the [people](/1.platform/10.objects/03.person.md) connected to it. See [billing collaboration](/1.platform/20.collaboration/3.billing-collaboration.md).

## Use cases

The invited person can be a client or a developer, see [account types](/1.platform/20.collaboration/7.account-types.md). Typical cases:

- A co-founder of a startup or agency
- Someone from the billing department
- A developer who manages billing

Connected people get the invoices and can change the payment details. Developers can also use the payment method for new or existing apps.

## How it works

The inviter sends the invite, the invited person accepts it.

### On the inviter side

1. The inviter needs access to the [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md).
2. Open the payment method invite in the dashboard.
3. Pick the payment method.
4. Enter the e-mail address and send.

:BlockLink{title="Invite to payment method" path="/invite/payment-method"}

### On the invited side

1. An e-mail with an invite link arrives.
2. Log in to fortrabbit, or create an account.
3. Accept the invite.

### Consequences

- The invited person gets a [billing connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/5.billing-connection.md) to the apps of the payment method, with the right to delete them or change their developers.
- The inviter keeps access.

## Alternatives

- To hand over the billing of apps instead of sharing it, use [invite to pay](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/12.invite-to-pay.md).
- A payment method of a team is shared with all members with the unlimited [role](/1.platform/20.collaboration/8.team-roles.md).
