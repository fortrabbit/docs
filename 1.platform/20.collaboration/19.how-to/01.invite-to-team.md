## Use cases

A [team](/1.platform/10.objects/04.team.md) shares current and future apps with a group of developers. Invite people you trust.

## How it works

The inviter sends the invite, the invited developer accepts it.

### On the inviter side

1. The inviter needs the unlimited role in the [team](/1.platform/10.objects/04.team.md).
2. Open the team invite in the dashboard and pick the team.
3. Choose the [role](/1.platform/20.collaboration/8.team-roles.md) of the new member.
4. Choose the apps to connect, see [developer connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/6.developer-connection.md).
5. Enter the e-mail address and send.

:BlockLink{title="Invite to team" path="/invite/team"}

### On the invited side

1. An e-mail with an invite link arrives.
2. Log in to fortrabbit, or create an account.
3. Accept the invite.

### Consequences

The new member joins the team with the chosen [role](/1.platform/20.collaboration/8.team-roles.md), which sets their access.

## Alternative app invite

To share single apps only, use the [app invite](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/09.invite-to-app.md).
