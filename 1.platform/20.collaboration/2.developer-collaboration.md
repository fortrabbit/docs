## Direct and team access

See the [collaboration intro](/1.platform/20.collaboration/1.intro.md) for the basics.

```raw
┌───────────┐    ┌───────────┐
│ developer │    │ developer │
└─────┬─────┘    └─────┬─────┘
  unlimited         limited
┌─────▼────────────────▼────┐
│           team            │
└───┬─────────┬─────────┬───┘
┌───▼───┐ ┌───▼───┐ ┌───▼───┐
│  app  │ │  app  │ │  app  │
└───────┘ └───▲───┘ └───────┘
        ┌─────┴─────┐
        │ developer │
        └───────────┘
```

Developers connect to apps directly, or through a team.

## Direct app access and collaboration

Apps created in the personal context belong to the account directly, which fits freelancers and personal projects. Share single apps with other developers by [app invite](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/09.invite-to-app.md).

## Team collaboration

A team shares a group of apps with a group of developers, with a role per member:

- [Team roles](/1.platform/20.collaboration/8.team-roles.md)
