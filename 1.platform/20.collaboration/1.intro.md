## Personal access

Each account belongs to one person, not to a company. One account is enough for most, see [account organization](/1.platform/03.concepts/01.account-organization.md) and the [person](/1.platform/10.objects/03.person.md).

## Two levels of collaboration

- [Developer collaboration](/1.platform/20.collaboration/2.developer-collaboration.md) shares the technical access to apps.
- [Billing collaboration](/1.platform/20.collaboration/3.billing-collaboration.md) shares access to payment methods.

A developer can work on an app without seeing its billing, and a client can pay for it without technical access. In a [team](/1.platform/10.objects/04.team.md), members with the unlimited role have both.
