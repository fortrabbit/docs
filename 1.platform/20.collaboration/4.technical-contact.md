A technical contact is a [person](/1.platform/10.objects/03.person.md) with a [developer connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/6.developer-connection.md) to the app, usually the developer who knows the code best. Choose the technical contacts in the settings of the [app](/1.platform/10.objects/01.app.md), from the developers connected to it. The developer who creates the app starts as one.

fortrabbit e-mails the technical contacts about matters such as:

- Software vulnerabilities
- Important software updates or incompatibilities
- Targeted DDoS attacks
- Limits about to be reached, see [autoscaling](/1.platform/12.billing/11.autoscaling.md)
- Suspicious activities
- Website hacked

These e-mails are best effort, without a guarantee to cover every problem. Keeping the software and the list of technical contacts up to date stays with the customer.
