```plain
# Billing connection
person → payment method → app
person → team → payment method → app
```

## What a billing connection allows

On the apps the payment method pays for:

- Open the app overview, without Git settings, branches and the description
- See the developers of the app, without changing their access
- Change the [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md) of the app
- Disconnect a team from the app
- Delete the app

On the payment method itself:

- [Invite](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/11.invite-to-payment-method.md) people to it
- Disconnect people and teams from it, as long as one billing connection remains

Developers with a billing connection can also connect themselves to the app as a developer and [invite a client to pay](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/12.invite-to-pay.md). A billing connection gives no code access, no deployments and no environment settings.

## Who can have a billing connection?

Developers and clients, see [account types](/1.platform/20.collaboration/7.account-types.md).

## Billing connection types

### Direct billing connection

A person on a payment method has a billing connection to its apps. Payment methods are shared by [invite](/1.platform/20.collaboration/19.how-to/11.invite-to-payment-method.md).

### Team billing connection

Team members with the unlimited [role](/1.platform/20.collaboration/8.team-roles.md) have a billing connection through every payment method connected to the team. Limited members have none.

## Relation to developer connection

The [developer connection](/1.platform/20.collaboration/6.developer-connection.md) is the technical counterpart. A developer can hold both on the same app.
