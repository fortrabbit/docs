## Understand individual access

An account belongs to one [person](/1.platform/10.objects/03.person.md), see [account organization](/1.platform/03.concepts/01.account-organization.md). The type is chosen at sign-up and can be changed later in the account settings.

## Developer

A developer account has the full dashboard, with technical control over [apps](/1.platform/10.objects/01.app.md), environments and [teams](/1.platform/10.objects/04.team.md). Developers can:

- Create and delete teams
- Collaborate in teams
- Create, delete and change apps
- Create, delete and change environments
- Deploy code to environments
- Create, delete and change payment methods
- Change billing for apps

## Client

A client account hides the technical parts of the dashboard. It suits clients of freelancers and agencies, finance managers and billing contacts. Clients can:

- Add and remove developers and teams to apps
- Delete apps
- Create, delete and change payment methods
- Change billing for apps

### Convenience

Clients manage their payment details and invoices themselves, without a developer in between.

### Responsibility

The client is the customer of fortrabbit. When the service fails, fortrabbit answers to the client directly, under the service level agreement. When a payment fails, fortrabbit follows up with the client, not with the developer.

### When it does not fit

Client accounts do not fit every case. Some companies do not allow accounts with outside providers, and some agencies bill hosting as part of a retainer. Then the agency pays with its own [payment method](/1.platform/10.objects/09.payment-method.md).
