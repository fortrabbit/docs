## About components

With a VPS, the size of the box sets the price, and every service runs inside it. On fortrabbit, the services run in separate containers, and each one is a component, booked and scaled per [environment](/1.platform/10.objects/02.environment.md).

PHP, MySQL, storage and traffic are components. Each comes in plans named like t-shirt sizes, XS, SM, MD, LG and up, with their own memory, size or volume. A PHP XS with a MySQL MD is a valid combination.

The [components list](/1.platform/09.components/index.md) shows all components and their plans, the [pricing structure](/1.platform/12.billing/01.pricing-structure.md) how they are billed, and the [pricing page](https://www.fortrabbit.com/pricing) the prices.

## Required and optional components

PHP, storage, traffic and backups are part of every environment. MySQL and jobs are optional.

## Components during the free trial

All components can be booked during the [free trial](/1.platform/02.get-started/02.free-trial.md), in all sizes. The largest plans need a payment method.
