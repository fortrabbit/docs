## ENV vars from the template

Version 6 of the Craft CMS [software template](/1.platform/03.concepts/05.software-templates.md) sets the Laravel and database [ENV vars](/1.platform/07.settings/01.env-vars.md):

:SoftwarePreset{slug="craft-cms" version="6"}

The `${FORTRABBIT_…}` values are [platform variables](/1.platform/07.settings/01.env-vars.md) that fortrabbit fills in. An app created without the template needs them added by hand:

:BlockLink{title="Edit ENV vars for {{app-name}} / {{app-env-name}}" path="/environments/{{app-env-id}}/env-vars"}

## Differences to Craft 5

- `DB_CONNECTION=mysql` is needed: the `.env.example` of the Craft 6 skeleton sets `pgsql`.
- `APP_KEY` replaces `CRAFT_SECURITY_KEY`. The template generates a random one. Data encrypted locally needs the local key on fortrabbit too.
- `APP_URL` sets the URLs of the Control Panel. Without it, links point to `localhost`.
- `LOG_STACK=stderr` sends the logs to the [logs](/1.platform/07.settings/25.logs.md) of the environment.

Settings in `config/craft/general.php` read ENV vars with Laravel's `env()` helper.
