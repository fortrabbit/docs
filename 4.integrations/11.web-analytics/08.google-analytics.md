## About Google Analytics

[Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com) is a web analytics service from Google. The current version, Google Analytics 4 (GA4), uses an event-based data model.

## Privacy considerations

Google Analytics sets cookies and processes personal data, which in the EU and other places needs consent from the visitor. Privacy-focused services such as [Fathom](/4.integrations/11.web-analytics/02.fathom.md) or [Plausible](/4.integrations/11.web-analytics/03.plausible.md) avoid cookies.

## Pricing

Google Analytics is free. Google Analytics 360 is the paid version for large companies.

## Integration

Add the Google tag from the Analytics account to the templates. fortrabbit needs no setup.
