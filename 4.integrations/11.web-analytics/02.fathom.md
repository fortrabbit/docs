## About Fathom

[Fathom Analytics](https://usefathom.com) counts visits without cookies, does not follow visitors across websites and stores no personal data. Paul Jarvis and Jack Ellis founded it in 2018 as an alternative to Google Analytics.

## Pricing

Fathom is a hosted service with a free trial and plans by page views, see [Fathom pricing](https://usefathom.com/pricing).

## Integration

Add the script from the Fathom dashboard to the templates. fortrabbit needs no setup.
