## About Rybbit

[Rybbit](https://rybbit.com) counts visits, sessions and events without cookies and without storing personal data. The code is open source under the AGPL, see [Rybbit on GitHub](https://github.com/rybbit-io/rybbit).

## Pricing

The hosted service starts with a 7-day trial, then plans by monthly page views, see [Rybbit pricing](https://rybbit.com/pricing). Self-hosting is free and needs a server with Docker, not fortrabbit.

## Integration

Add the script from the Rybbit dashboard to the templates of the website. fortrabbit needs no setup.
