## About Plausible

[Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io) counts visits without cookies and stores no personal data. Its code is open source.

## Pricing

The hosted service has a free trial and plans by page views, see [Plausible pricing](https://plausible.io/#pricing). The self-hosted Community Edition is free, and runs on a server with Docker, not on fortrabbit.

## Integration

Add the script from the Plausible dashboard to the templates. fortrabbit needs no setup.
