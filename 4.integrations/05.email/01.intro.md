## About email accounts

fortrabbit provides neither mailboxes nor an SMTP server. Two cases need a provider:

1. **Mailboxes** (IMAP), see [e-mail providers](/4.integrations/05.email/02.providers.md)
2. **Mail sent by the app**, see [transactional mail](/4.integrations/10.transactional-mail/01.intro.md)

## Integration with fortrabbit

The MX records of the domain point to the mail provider. fortrabbit does not touch them: pointing the website to fortrabbit changes only the A and CNAME records, see the [external domain setup](/1.platform/13.domains/04.external-domain-setup.md).
