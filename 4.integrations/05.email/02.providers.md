## Email account provider types

- Some [domain providers](/4.integrations/02.dns/02.providers.md) include mailboxes with the domain.
- Dedicated mail services add office apps, team management and backups.

Compare privacy, the country where the mail is stored, and the price per mailbox.

## Some email account providers

Providers popular with fortrabbit clients:

- [Google Workspace](https://workspace.google.com/) - Full-featured office suite with Gmail for your domain.
- [Namecheap Email](https://www.namecheap.com/hosting/email.aspx) - Email offering from domain provider.
- [Proton Mail](https://proton.me/mail) - Encrypted mail from Switzerland.
- [Fastmail](https://www.fastmail.com) - Independent mail service from Australia.
- [Zoho Mail](https://www.zoho.com/mail) - Office solution with free entry option.
- [Runbox](https://runbox.com) - Secure mail from Norway.

fortrabbit has no business relation with these providers, and the links are not affiliate links.
