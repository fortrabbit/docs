APM tools trace single requests through the code, to the slow function, query or call to another service. The [metrics](/1.platform/26.metrics/index.md) of fortrabbit show that something got slow, and when. An APM tool shows where in the code.

## APM on fortrabbit

Two APM extensions are installed and switched on per environment in the [PHP settings](/1.platform/07.settings/06.php.md):

- [Blackfire](/4.integrations/04.apm/02.blackfire.md), a profiler
- [New Relic](/4.integrations/04.apm/03.new-relic.md), monitoring of all requests

Other APM tools that need an extension of their own do not run on fortrabbit. Tools that work as a Composer package, such as Sentry, do.

## Relation to web analytics

APM measures the code, [web analytics](/4.integrations/11.web-analytics/01.intro.md) the visitors. Some tools, such as Sentry and PostHog, cover parts of both.

## Local profiling

[PHP SPX](https://github.com/NoiseByNorthwest/php-spx) is an open source profiler. It needs its own extension, which fortrabbit does not have, so use it in the [local development environment](/4.integrations/01.local-development/01.intro.md).
