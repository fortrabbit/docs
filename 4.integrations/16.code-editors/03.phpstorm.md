[PhpStorm](https://www.jetbrains.com/phpstorm/) understands Laravel, Symfony and other frameworks, with completion and navigation into framework code. Database client, HTTP client and terminal are built in. We use it at fortrabbit, with no business relation to JetBrains.

## Price

PhpStorm is a yearly or monthly subscription, with a 30-day free trial. Free licenses exist for students and teachers, and for open-source projects that are not used commercially.

## Performance

PhpStorm runs on the JVM. Startup and the first indexing of a project take a while; after that the IDE is fast.

## AI

JetBrains AI, with the AI Assistant and the Junie coding agent, comes with a free quota in the IDE. Larger quotas need an AI Pro or AI Ultimate subscription. Local models through Ollama or LM Studio keep code on the computer.

## Privacy

Sharing anonymous usage statistics is opt-in, under **Settings → Appearance & Behavior → System Settings → Data Sharing**. Code leaves the computer only with cloud AI features or plugins turned on.
