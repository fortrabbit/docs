## About UptimeRobot

[UptimeRobot](https://uptimerobot.com/) checks websites, keywords, ports and cron heartbeats, and includes status pages.

## Pricing

A free plan for personal use with 5-minute checks, paid plans with shorter intervals, see [UptimeRobot pricing](https://uptimerobot.com/pricing/).

## Integration

Add the domains in UptimeRobot. fortrabbit needs no setup.
