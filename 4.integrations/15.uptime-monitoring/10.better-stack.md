## About Better Stack

[Better Stack](https://betterstack.com) monitors uptime, handles on-call and incidents, hosts status pages, and collects logs. The [fortrabbit status page](https://fortrabbit.betteruptime.com/) runs on Better Stack.

## Pricing

Free for personal projects, paid plans per team member and usage, see [Better Stack pricing](https://betterstack.com/pricing).

## Integration

Add the domains as monitors in Better Stack. fortrabbit needs no setup.
