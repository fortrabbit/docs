## About Instatus

[Instatus](https://instatus.com/) hosts status pages, monitors uptime and posts incident updates to subscribers.

## Pricing

A free plan and paid plans with more monitors, see [Instatus pricing](https://instatus.com/pricing).

## Integration

Add the domains as monitors in Instatus. fortrabbit needs no setup.
