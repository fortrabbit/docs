## About Uptime Kuma

[Uptime Kuma](https://uptimekuma.org/) checks HTTP, keywords, ports, DNS and more, with notifications to many chat services and public status pages. The source is on GitHub: [louislam/uptime-kuma](https://github.com/louislam/uptime-kuma).

## Pricing

Free and open source. It needs a server of its own.

## Integration

Uptime Kuma is a Node.js app and runs on a server with Docker, not on fortrabbit. Monitor the fortrabbit domains from there, ideally from another provider than the one hosting the website.
