## About Oh Dear

[Oh Dear](https://ohdear.app) checks websites every minute: uptime, certificates, broken links, mixed content, DNS and scheduled tasks. Freek Van der Herten and Mattias Geniar, both from the PHP community, founded it in 2018.

## Pricing

Plans by number of sites, with a 10-day free trial, see [Oh Dear pricing](https://ohdear.app/pricing).

## Integration

Add the domains in Oh Dear. The scheduled task checks need a ping from the cron, see [crons](/1.platform/30.jobs/02.crons.md).
