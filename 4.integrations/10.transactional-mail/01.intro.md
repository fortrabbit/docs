This page covers e-mail sent by the app. For mailboxes on an own domain, see [e-mail hosting](/4.integrations/05.email/01.intro.md).

## No sendmail

PHP's [mail function](https://www.php.net/manual/en/function.mail.php) hands the mail to a local sendmail, which fortrabbit does not have, see [limits](/1.platform/03.concepts/09.limits.md#mailing). This does not send:

```php
$to      = 'firstname.lastname@gmail.com';
$subject = 'Welcome to our service';
$message = 'hello, nice to have you here …';
$headers = 'From: webmaster@mycoolapp.com'

mail($to, $subject, $message, $headers);
```

Mail sent straight from a web server, without the SPF and DKIM records of a real mail server, ends up in spam or is rejected by the large mail providers anyway.

## About transactional emails

Transactional e-mail is sent by the app to one person, triggered by an action: a sign-up confirmation, a password reset, an invoice. Transactional mail providers deliver it by SMTP or API, help with the SPF and DKIM records of the domain, and log every message with bounces and opens. The SMTP ports are open in the [outgoing firewall](/1.platform/07.settings/21.firewall-outgoing.md). See [providers](/4.integrations/10.transactional-mail/02.providers.md).
