Well-known providers:

- [Amazon SES](https://aws.amazon.com/ses)
- [Postmark](https://postmarkapp.com) - see [article](/4.integrations/10.transactional-mail/03.postmark.md)
- [SendGrid](https://sendgrid.com) - see [article](/4.integrations/10.transactional-mail/04.sendgrid.md)
- [Mailgun](https://www.mailgun.com)
- [Mailjet](https://www.mailjet.com)
- [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com)

Smaller ones:

- [Doppler Relay](https://www.dopplerrelay.com)
- [GreenArrow](https://www.greenarrowemail.com)
- [Netcore Email API](https://netcore.ai/email/email-api/), formerly Pepipost
- [SocketLabs](https://www.socketlabs.com)
- [Bird Email API](https://bird.com/email-api), formerly SparkPost

[Mailtrap](https://mailtrap.io) catches the mail of a test environment in a fake inbox, so nothing reaches real people. fortrabbit sends its own mail with Postmark and has no other business relation with these services. No affiliate links.

## Self hosted

An own mail server gives full control, at the cost of running it and keeping its IP addresses off spam lists.

- [Postal](https://docs.postalserver.io/), an open source mail server for sending and receiving.
