[RunsOn](https://runs-on.com/) installs into your AWS account, control plane included. It supports arm64 Graviton instances, GPU instances and your own AMIs. Commercial use needs a yearly license priced by launched runners; non-commercial use is free.

## Deploy to fortrabbit

The runner builds and tests. The deployment step connects over SSH with a [deploy key](/1.platform/10.objects/12.ssh-key.md#deploy-keys), see [GitHub Actions runners](/4.integrations/12.github-actions-runners/01.intro.md).
