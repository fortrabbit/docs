[WinSCP](https://winscp.net/) shows local and remote files side by side and has a built-in text editor.

## Setup

1. Open WinSCP.
2. **File protocol**: Select `SFTP`.
3. **Host name**: `ssh.{{region}}.frbit.app`
4. **Port number**: `22`
5. **User name**: `{{app-env-id}}`
6. **Password**: Leave blank.
7. Click **Advanced...** > **SSH** > **Authentication**.
8. **Private key file**: Browse and select the private key. WinSCP offers to convert an OpenSSH key to its `.ppk` format.
9. Click **OK** to close Advanced settings.
10. Click **Login**.

Save the session to reuse it.
